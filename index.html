About

Fashion is identity, atmosphere and storytelling. It should say something without a word.

I’m Ace, a 6’0” lean-muscular editorial model and fashion creator. Acesthetics is where dark contemporary menswear, tailoring, leather and editorial streetwear meet.

My work is often faceless on purpose. Silhouette, pose, light and composition carry the mood, so the clothes and the atmosphere stay in focus. I model for other creators’ shoots and direct my own, always aiming for looks that feel masculine, refined and understated.
